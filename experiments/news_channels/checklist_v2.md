# News-channel checklist v2 — «keys of influence» (agreed 22.10.2026; code: `checklist_v2.py`)

Two goals of the news classification: (1) better retrieval of news for insights (news-first candidate generator,
noise filter); (2) ideas for the insight agent — through which channel exactly an event can hit this business.

Agreed: hierarchy (rankers score leaves, parent = max of its leaves; company matching, factory v2 and GLM labels stay
on parents), sink item, splits of G1 / A2 / B1 / C3, merges D1+D2, A3+C4, C1+C2. New reasons were checked against the
existing items: most are already covered and only sharpen a leaf's wording; three are new (marked NEW).
Each leaf lists what it must not be confused with («≠»).
